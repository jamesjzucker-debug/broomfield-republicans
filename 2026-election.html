2026.
The Year We Take It Back.

Governor. State Senate. State House. School board. Every race on the 2026 ballot is a chance to flip the script. Here's what's on the line, and how you fight back.

Key dates

Mark your calendar.

These are the dates every Broomfield Republican should have on the fridge. We'll update this timeline as the Colorado Secretary of State confirms each milestone.

Dates shown are illustrative for layout. Confirm with the Colorado Secretary of State before sharing publicly.

March 2026

Caucus Night

Republicans across Broomfield gather by precinct to elect delegates and discuss party business. Open only to registered Republicans.

April 2026

County & State Assemblies

Delegates select candidates for the primary ballot. The path to November starts here.

June 2026

Primary Election

All registered voters, including unaffiliated, receive a primary ballot in the mail. Choose Republican to help pick our nominees.

November 2026

General Election

The big one. Governor, Congress, State Legislature, ballot measures, and local races on the line.
