What to expect

A different kind of political dinner.

We've all sat through political dinners that felt like homework. This isn't that. Liberty & Laughs is built around a clean, sharp comedy set, a great meal, and a real conversation with a Colorado statewide candidate, all in two and a half hours. Come with your spouse. Come with your skeptical neighbor. Come with the friend who's always meant to get involved.

First time at a BCRC event?

You'll feel right at home.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Who can attend?

Anyone is welcome. Most attendees will be Broomfield Republicans, but the event is intentionally open to neighbors, friends from Adams, Jefferson, Boulder, and Weld counties, and anyone curious about getting involved. Bring a spouse, bring a friend, bring a skeptic.

Who is the special guest?

A 2026 Colorado GOP statewide candidate. We'll announce the exact guest about four weeks before the event. Keep an eye on the BCRC email list and our social channels. The format will be a short on-stage conversation, not a long speech.

Who is the comedian?

A regional headliner with a clean set. We pre-vet material before booking. We'll announce the comedian closer to the event.

What's included with my ticket?

Dinner, the program with the special guest, the comedy set, and access to the silent auction. Cash bar. Tables of 8 include the same for everyone at the table at a $20 savings versus eight individual tickets.

Are tickets refundable?

Tickets are non-refundable but are transferable. If you can't make it, give yours to a friend and let us know who's coming in your place. Email Chairman@BroomfieldRepublicans.org with the substitution.

Are contributions tax-deductible?

No. Contributions to a political party committee are not deductible as charitable contributions for federal income tax purposes. Colorado law caps aggregate contributions from any one person to a county political party committee at $4,675 per calendar year. The committee collects name, address, occupation, and employer for any contribution over $20.

I'd like to sponsor but can't decide on a level, can someone call me?

Absolutely. Email Chairman@BroomfieldRepublicans.org with a phone number and a good time, and a member of the host committee will be in touch.
