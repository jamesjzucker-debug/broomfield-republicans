How to Vote in Broomfield

Ballots were mailed October 2. Didn't get one? Call the Broomfield Elections Division at 303-464-5857.

7 drop boxes, open 24 hours through 7 p.m. on Election Day, including the George Di Ciero City and County Building (One DesCombes Dr.) and the Paul Derda Rec Center.

In-person voting at the Di Ciero Building starts October 19. Four voting centers are open 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. on November 3.

Mailing it back? Do it by October 26. After that, use a drop box.

Vote Yes

  • Amendment 81: Police notify federal immigration authorities about certain criminal suspects
  • Amendment 82: Constitutional right to buy and sell natural gas for home & business
  • Amendment 83: Constitutional right to hunt & fish
  • Amendment 84: Requires voter ID on mail ballots
  • Amendment 85: Requires clear, plain-language ballot titles & summaries
  • Amendment 86: Limits off-cycle congressional redistricting
  • Proposition 132: Increases penalties for fentanyl crimes
  • Proposition 133: Life sentences for human trafficking of a minor
  • Proposition 134: School & college sports teams based on biological sex
  • Proposition 135: Prohibits surgery on a minor to alter biological sex
  • Proposition 136: Caps Colorado's state income tax rate at 4.4%

Vote No

  • Amendment 87: Replaces Colorado's flat 4.4% income tax with graduated rates from 3.7% to 8.4%
  • Proposition NN: Lets the state keep money that would otherwise come back to you as TABOR refunds
  • Proposition 137: Moves sporting-goods sales tax revenue outside TABOR, shrinking refunds

On every Broomfield ballot

  • Broomfield 1A: Lodging tax jumps from 1.6% to 6%, up to $3.3 million a year
  • Charter Amendment 1C: Stretches mayoral terms to 4 years
  • Front Range Passenger Rail 7A: $295 million a year sales tax increase, plus $580 million in debt
  • 17th Judicial District 7E: Loosens the DA's term limit from two terms to three

On some Broomfield ballots

  • Jeffco School District 5A: $73 million tax increase
  • Jeffco School District 5B: $60 million tax increase
  • Weld RE-8 School District 5C: $4.9 million tax increase, plus extending $2.7 million in expiring taxes

Broomfield 1B, the 20-year Xcel Energy franchise renewal, is also on your ballot. It raises no tax. BCR takes no position.
