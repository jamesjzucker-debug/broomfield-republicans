Straight With You

Where the support goes.

Every ticket you buy goes to the Broomfield County Republicans to fund community events like this one, voter outreach, and local organizing. It's a fun way to support the work while giving yourself a shot at a great prize.

Raffle ticket purchases to a county political party are capped by Colorado law (about $4,675 per person per year) and are not tax-deductible.

The Fine Print

Rules, briefly.

Tickets are $5 each or 5 for $20. One prize is awarded per drawing. Open to legal Colorado residents 18 and older. Prizes are accessories and merchandise only — no firearm is ever awarded or transferred. Need not be present to win; the winner is contacted by email or phone after the drawing and is responsible for any applicable taxes. All range activity follows Shoot Indoors safety rules and the signed waiver. Void where prohibited.

Questions? Email Chairman@BroomfieldRepublicans.org. Full official rules are available on request.
