Monthly Meeting · Every 2nd Monday

BGOP Monthly Meeting

Open to all registered Republicans in Broomfield County. Updates from officers, candidate appearances, county business, and Q&A. Same night, same place, every month.

When
2nd Monday · 6:30 PM
Where
Broomfield Community Center · 280 Spader Way

Meet upstairs. No need to RSVP, just come. Guest speakers are announced on the calendar below and in the weekly community update.

Social · Thu, August 20

Community Range Night

The Second Amendment in action. Know your rights, and practice them with good neighbors. A relaxed evening at the range with first-timers welcome and certified instructors on hand. No experience or party membership required.

When
Thu, August 20 · 6–9 PM
Where
Shoot Indoors · 1 Park St, Broomfield

Ladies Night pricing: women shoot $10, men $26. We cover gun rentals and bring the pizza. Rental firearms, eye and ear protection, and targets are on site. Just bring a friend and a photo ID.
