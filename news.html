On the ballot · November 2, 2027

Six seats. Every ward is in play.

Broomfield is a consolidated city and county, so one Council runs all of it: the budget, growth and land use, taxes and public safety. Next November voters fill the Mayor's seat and one Council seat in each of the five wards.

Citywide

Mayor

Elected at large by every voter in Broomfield for a two-year term. The Mayor runs Council meetings and is the public face of city government.

Wards 1 through 5

City Council

One seat in each ward, each for a four-year term. You run in the ward you live in and have lived in for at least a year before Election Day.

City races are nonpartisan, so no party label shows up next to your name on the ballot. Your values still do. Details per the Broomfield Elections Division, as of November 2026.
